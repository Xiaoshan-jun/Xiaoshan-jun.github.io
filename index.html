I am a Ph.D. candidate in the joint Mechanical Engineering program at UC San Diego and San Diego State University, advised by Prof. Jun Chen and Prof. Sonia Martínez. I expect to graduate in 2026.

My research focuses on safe and stable learning-based control for autonomous systems. I develop reinforcement-learning and neural-network controllers, formal verification methods, probabilistic reachability analysis, trajectory prediction models, and risk-aware motion-planning algorithms.
